2. Data and demerger methodology

Pre-demerger TMPV-file prices are treated as retrospectively adjusted. The workbook reconstructs a reference old Tata Motors price by dividing the TMPV file price by 0.6054, then allocates that reference price using the selected cost-of-acquisition split: 68.85% TMPV and 31.15% TMCV.

5. Price versus fundamentals

FY-end adjusted Combined price can be considered alongside revenue, PAT and total assets only where the relevant price and financial observations exist. The combined market-price series has a post-demerger gap until both actual entity prices are available; therefore, missing FY-end values are displayed as unavailable rather than estimated.

6. Conclusion

The financial statements show a recovery from losses in FY21 and FY22 to positive reported profitability in FY23–FY25, alongside higher revenue and asset turnover. FY26 requires separate treatment because of the PV/CV reporting split, the exceptional gain and the absence of inter-company eliminations in the analytical Combined total.

Price comparisons should be interpreted in the context of the demerger and the different observation windows for the two listed entities. The post-demerger TMCV series is shorter than the TMPV series, so the two should not be treated as equal-length samples.

  • Nifty Auto is a price index, not a TRI; dividends are excluded.
  • TMCV actual price observations start 25-Nov-2025.
  • The analysis focuses on financial ratios that can be derived consistently from the available annual statements.
  • Blank financial statement values are preserved and are not treated as zero.
